About this map

I made Bangkok, Unmarked after coming across Dern.Collective on Instagram, a page that reminded me how much history is packed into every corner of the city I grew up in.

When I moved to the US for college, people would tell me they wanted to visit Bangkok, and I would tell them it's amazing, without fully grasping how true that was myself. It wasn't until recently that I understood how much depth this city holds, in the street where I meet a friend for lunch, in the alley I pass on my way to shop.

This project is my way of documenting that: a love letter to the city I never want to neglect, and to the stories I never want to be forgotten.
